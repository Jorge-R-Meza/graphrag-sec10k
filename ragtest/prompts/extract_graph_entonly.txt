-Goal-
Given a text document that is potentially relevant to this activity and a list of entity types, identify ALL entities of those types from the text in order to capture the information and ideas in the text. Relationships between entities are extracted in a later, separate step — do NOT extract them here. Spend your entire output on entities: it is better to emit one entity too many than to miss one.

-Steps-
1. Identify all entities. For each identified entity, extract the following information:
- entity_name: Name of the entity, capitalized. For companies, use the common name without legal suffixes (APPLE, not APPLE INC.). Always attribute first-person text ("we", "our", "the Company") to the named filer of the document. Name events with a distinguishing party or date (SECURITIES CLASS ACTION (MARCH 2024), not LITIGATION).
- entity_type: One of the following types: [{entity_types}]. Do not invent new types — every entity must use exactly one of these.
- entity_description: Comprehensive description of the entity's attributes and activities. Preserve exact figures, percentages, dollar amounts, and fiscal years VERBATIM from the text (e.g. "accounted for approximately 59% of net revenue in fiscal 2025", not "a majority of revenue").
When the text defines a composite figure or breakdown (e.g. "four contract manufacturers each accounted for greater than 10% of production and approximately 57% in the aggregate", or "Vietnam, Indonesia and China manufactured approximately 50%, 27% and 18% of footwear, respectively"), reproduce the FULL breakdown VERBATIM inside the relevant entity_description, and also extract each named component (each country, each named party) as its own entity with its exact share stated in its description.
Format each entity as ("entity"<|>NAME<|>TYPE<|>DESCRIPTION)

2. Output ONLY entity records — do NOT output relationship records of any kind.

3. Return output in English as a single list of all the entities identified in step 1. Use **##** as the list delimiter.

4. If you have to translate into English, just translate the descriptions, nothing else!

5. When finished, output <|COMPLETE|>.

-Type definitions-
Every entity must be assigned exactly one of these types. Use these definitions strictly:

- organization: a SPECIFIC named company, government agency, court, or institution (APPLE, TSMC, INTERNAL REVENUE SERVICE, U.S. DISTRICT COURT FOR THE NORTHERN DISTRICT OF CALIFORNIA). Unnamed categories ("our contract manufacturers", "OEM customers", "our competitors", "distributors") are NOT organizations — do not extract unnamed categories at all.
- person: a named individual (an officer, director, or founder).
- geo: a SPECIFIC named place — a country, state, city, or region (VIETNAM, TAIWAN, SINGAPORE). Categorical references ("Asia-Pacific markets", "foreign jurisdictions") are not geo entities.
- product: a SPECIFIC named product, brand, platform, or technology family (SNAPDRAGON, NIKE BRAND FOOTWEAR, WI-FI 7 FRONT-END MODULES when named as a product line). Generic commodity categories ("semiconductors", "smartphones") are not products.
- business segment: a reportable segment or operating division AS NAMED by the filer (QCT, QUALCOMM TECHNOLOGY LICENSING, ANALOG SIGNAL CHAINS).
- financial metric: a measurable quantity, ratio, or indicator with its value when stated (NET REVENUE, GROSS MARGIN, CUSTOMER CONCENTRATION, R&D EXPENSE).
- debt instrument: a specific named debt obligation or facility (2031 SENIOR NOTES, REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY, COMMERCIAL PAPER PROGRAM).
- regulation: a named law, rule, standard, tariff, or government program the company is subject to or benefits from (GDPR, SECURITIES EXCHANGE ACT OF 1934, CHIPS ACT, SECTION 301 TARIFFS, EXPORT ADMINISTRATION REGULATIONS). It exists independently of the company.
- event: a specific discrete occurrence involving the company — a lawsuit, investigation, acquisition, divestiture, restructuring, impairment charge, or leadership transition — named with a party or date anchor. Boilerplate ("various legal proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business") is NOT an event; extract nothing for it.
